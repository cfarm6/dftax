## Explicitly verified non-findings (evidence, not repetition)

- Local-hybrid (position-dependent EXX mixing) presence: NOT established either way by flags — `HYB_LC/LCY` are deprecated CAM aliases (see `src/xc.h:68-71` above), so their absence proves nothing. Positive evidence against EXX-density ingredients: every SymPy kernel signature at the pin has arity 2 (`rs,zeta`), 5 (`rs,z,xt,xs0,xs1`), or 9 (GGA 5 + `u0,u1,t0,t1` or `us0,us1,ts0,ts1`) — 27/112/136 across `python/*/*.py` — with no `ax/exx_dens` channel; Minnesota exchange kernels take the standard 9 (`mgga_x_m08.py:51`, `mgga_x_m11.py:28`); M08 init calls `xc_hyb_init_hybrid(p, 0.0)` ([`src/mgga_x_m08.c:42-48`](https://gitlab.com/libxc/libxc/-/blob/7d236789c2a4521270eeaa41d06e0d721ef56abd/src/mgga_x_m08.c)) with the fraction in ext params via `set_ext_params_cpy_exx`, i.e. scalar, not density-mixing.
